# Trademark registration

## Summary
A trademark is a mark used, or proposed to be used, on goods to show a trade connection between those goods and a person or business (Trade Marks Act, section 67). Registering a trademark gives the owner the exclusive right to use it for the registered goods or services. The rights can last indefinitely, as long as the registration is renewed. (TM-1) Registration is territorial: it only protects the mark in Nigeria. (TM-3)

### Why our business types need it
- **Packaged fruit drinks:** NAFDAC lists evidence of trademark registration as a required document for product registration. (See the NAFDAC food and drinks document.)
- **Online cosmetics store, import own brand and standard local manufacture:** NAFDAC lists evidence of brand name registration with the Trademarks Registry as a required document. At NAFDAC micro scale it is only advised. (See the NAFDAC cosmetics document.)
- **Furniture business and retail pharmacy:** optional, to protect the brand name.

**Open question:** NAFDAC asks for "evidence of trademark registration". Whether the Registry's acceptance letter (T03) is enough, or the final certificate (T05) is required, is not stated in these sources. **Confirm with NAFDAC.** This decides whether trademark registration holds up NAFDAC registration for several months.

## Requirement: Trademark availability search
- **Requirement ID:** T01
- **Issued by:** Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, online cosmetics store (required for NAFDAC); furniture business, retail pharmacy (optional)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing
- **Must be done before:** trademark application (T02)
- **Why it comes first:** the Registry advises making sure a mark does not infringe an existing trademark before applying. An availability search checks the Registry's database for existing and pending marks. If the mark is available and registrable, the applicant can go ahead and apply. (TM-4)
- **Who can do it:** only an accredited agent of the Registry can conduct a search. A list of accredited agents is on the Registry's website. (TM-4)
- **Fee:** availability search ₦1,500; preliminary advice on availability ₦1,500. (TM-2)

## Requirement: Trademark application (filing)
- **Requirement ID:** T02
- **Issued by:** Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, online cosmetics store; furniture business, retail pharmacy (optional)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** availability search (T01)
- **Must be done before:** examination and acceptance (T03)

### What the mark must be (TM-1)
- Distinctive for the goods or services.
- Not deceptive, illegal or immoral, and not similar to an existing mark.
- **Colour:** a mark registered in specific colours is protected only in those colours. A mark registered in plain black and white is protected in all colours.

### Classes (TM-1, TM-3)
- Nigeria uses the Nice International Classification of Goods.
- A separate application is needed for each class of goods or services. Registration only protects the class or classes it was granted for.

### Information and documents needed (TM-1, TM-3)
- Applicant's full name or names, physical address, nationality, email and phone number
- A representation of the mark that is visible, clear and distinct. For online filing: a JPEG soft copy of at least 1200 dpi (preferred size 120 × 100 pixels).
- A power of attorney or authorisation of agent form, when filing through an agent. For a company, the full name and capacity of the person signing must be given to the agent and filed with the application.

### Who can file (TM-1, TM-3)
- Accredited trademark attorneys, agents, individuals, companies and firms can file applications on behalf of applicants or owners. (TM-3)
- Foreign applicants usually instruct a local attorney or agent in Nigeria. (TM-1)
- Online payments use pre-generated invoices obtained through a platform for local agents. (TM-1)
- Whether a Nigerian business owner can file directly, without an agent, is not clearly stated. **Unknown — confirm with the Registry.**

### Steps (TM-1)
1. File the application online. Payment is made by debit or credit card through Quickteller.
2. The Registrar issues an acknowledgement letter with the official application number and filing date. For online filing, the acknowledgement is sent on the day of payment.
3. Track the application with the online application ID in the Check Status module.

### Fee (TM-2)
- Application to register a trademark: ₦16,749 (a second entry on the same page lists ₦15,000). **Confirm which applies.** The fee is charged per application, and each class needs a separate application.

### Where to apply
- Registry portal: https://nipo.gov.ng (login: https://nipo.gov.ng/login.jsp; register: https://nipo.gov.ng/register.jsp)

## Requirement: Examination and acceptance
- **Requirement ID:** T03
- **Issued by:** Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, online cosmetics store; furniture business, retail pharmacy (optional)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** trademark application (T02)
- **Must be done before:** publication in the Trade Marks Journal (T04)
- **What happens (TM-1, TM-3):** the Registry searches existing and pending registrations to confirm the mark is available. It then examines whether the mark can be registered, including any conflict with earlier or pending marks. If the mark is acceptable, the applicant receives a **letter or notice of acceptance**.
- **Timeline:** not stated in the sources read. **Unknown.**

## Requirement: Publication and opposition period
- **Requirement ID:** T04
- **Issued by:** Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, online cosmetics store; furniture business, retail pharmacy (optional)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** examination and acceptance (T03)
- **Must be done before:** registration certificate (T05)
- **What happens (TM-1, TM-3):**
  - The accepted mark is advertised in the Nigerian Trade Marks Journal (journals: https://nipo.gov.ng/journals.jsp).
  - Anyone can oppose it for **two months** from the date of advertisement.
- **Opposition fees (TM-2):** notice of opposition ₦8,000; counter statement ₦8,000; statutory declaration ₦8,000.

## Requirement: Registration certificate
- **Requirement ID:** T05
- **Issued by:** Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, online cosmetics store; furniture business, retail pharmacy (optional)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** publication and opposition period (T04)
- **What happens (TM-1, TM-3):** if there is no opposition, or the opposition fails, the applicant pays the prescribed fee and the Registrar issues a certificate of registration.
- **Fee (TM-2):** application for a trademark certificate ₦11,529.80 (a second entry on the same page lists ₦10,000). **Confirm which applies.**
- **Certified true copy of the certificate:** ₦8,000. (TM-2)
- **Total time from filing to certificate:** not stated in the sources read. At least the two-month opposition period applies. **Unknown.**

## Requirement: Trademark renewal
- **Requirement ID:** T06
- **Issued by:** Trademarks, Patents and Designs Registry
- **Applies to:** any business with a registered trademark
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** registration certificate (T05)

### Details (TM-1, TM-2)
- **Validity:** the first registration lasts **seven years**. After that it is renewed every **fourteen years**, indefinitely.
- **When to apply:** at least three months before the current registration expires.
- **Renewal fee:** ₦14,909.64 (a second entry on the same page lists ₦12,000). **Confirm which applies.**
- **Late renewal penalty:** ₦5,000.
- **Certified true copy of a renewal certificate:** ₦8,000.
