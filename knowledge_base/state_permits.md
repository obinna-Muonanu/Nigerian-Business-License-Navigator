# State business premises permits

## Summary
Most Nigerian states require every business premises to be registered with the state government and the registration renewed every year. These are state requirements, separate from CAC and federal registrations. Each state has its own law, agency, fees and process.

### Which business types this applies to
- **Packaged fruit drinks:** yes, for the production premises.
- **Retail pharmacy:** yes, for the shop.
- **Furniture business:** yes, for the shop, showroom or workshop.
- **Online cosmetics store (no physical shop):** the sources read do not say whether an online-only business needs a premises permit. In Ogun and Edo the rule covers businesses "operating within" the state (SP-OG-2, SP-ED), so an office or storage space used by the business may be covered. **Unknown — confirm with the state agency.**

### State tax registration
State tax registration is covered in the tax document (nrs_tax_id.md). In Oyo, a tax clearance certificate is needed for premises registration (see S-OY-01).

| State | Premises permit issued by | Fees published | Coverage in this document |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oyo | Ministry of Trade, Industry, Investment and Cooperatives | Yes, full table | Full |
| Lagos | Lagos State Internal Revenue Service | Yes, statutory maximums | Good |
| FCT | FCT Internal Revenue Service | No | Partial (secondary source) |
| Rivers | Rivers State Internal Revenue Service | No | Limited |
| Ogun | Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment | Calculated by the online portal | Partial |
| Edo | Edo State Internal Revenue Service (ERAS) | No | Limited |

## Requirement: Oyo State business premises registration
- **Requirement ID:** S-OY-01
- **Issued by:** Oyo State Ministry of Trade, Industry, Investment and Cooperatives
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, retail pharmacy, furniture business (and an online cosmetics store with premises in Oyo — to confirm)
- **Applies in:** Oyo
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01); Oyo State tax registration (R02-ST-OY), because a personal income tax clearance certificate is a required document
- **Must be done before:** carrying on business at the premises

### The law (SP-OY)
- No one may carry on a business unless the business or professional premises is registered with the Ministry.
- Registration must be renewed every year.
- Authorised officers can enter premises at any reasonable time to check compliance.
- Premises whose owner refuses or evades the fee can be sealed, with police help. Carrying on business there, or removing the seal, is unlawful until the fee is paid or the seal is removed by the Commissioner or an authorised officer.
- Registration can be suspended or cancelled by written order if the law is not complied with.

### Documents needed (SP-OY)
- Written application to the Honourable Commissioner
- Copy of the business's CAC certificate
- Copies of personal income tax clearance certificates for the last three years
- Evidence of payment of the registration fee

A brand-new business may not have three years of tax clearance certificates. How the Ministry handles this is not stated. **Unknown — ask the Ministry.**

### Steps (SP-OY)
**Paying at a bank:**
1. Check the fee on the Ministry's notice board (or in the table below).
2. Pay at any commercial bank in Oyo State into the Oyo State Government account, under revenue code 4030009.
3. Collect the electronic revenue receipt and make two photocopies.
4. Confirm the receipt at the Treasury Office (Room 22, Office of the Accountant General).
5. Submit the application letter, receipt and other documents to the Commissioner's office.

**Paying online:**
1. Go to payment.oyostatebir.com and click "proceed to payment".
2. Choose the Ministry as the agency and "Business Premises Registration" as the revenue type.
3. Enter the amount, payment period and personal details, and pay through myxpresspay.
4. Print the electronic receipt and make two photocopies.
5. Confirm it at the Treasury Office (Room 22), then submit everything to the Commissioner's office.

### Timeline (SP-OY)
About 48 hours from submitting a complete application to receiving the certificate.

### Fees for our business types (SP-OY)
Fees depend on the business category and the zone. **Zone A** is Ibadan; **Zone B** is Oyo and Ogbomoso; **Zone C** is Ibarapa and Oke-Ogun. Each cell shows registration / annual renewal, in naira.

| Category in Oyo's table | Which of our businesses | Zone A (Ibadan) | Zone B | Zone C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manufacturing, small scale | Packaged fruit drinks (small producer) — likely category | 250,000 / 250,000 | 250,000 / 250,000 | 250,000 / 250,000 |
| Manufacturing, medium scale | Packaged fruit drinks (medium producer) | 500,000 / 500,000 | 500,000 / 500,000 | 500,000 / 500,000 |
| Artisans / petty traders | Possibly a very small home producer | 2,000 / 1,000 | 1,500 / 750 | 1,000 / 500 |
| Furniture and house fittings | Furniture business | 100,000 / 75,000 | 75,000 / 50,000 | 50,000 / 30,000 |
| Warehouse, small | Online cosmetics store with a storage space (if required) | 150,000 / 100,000 | 100,000 / 70,000 | 70,000 / 50,000 |

- **Retail pharmacy:** the table has no "pharmacy" category. The nearest categories are "hospitals and clinics" (Zone A ₦20,000 / ₦10,000) and "stores" (departmental stores Zone A ₦250,000 / ₦200,000). **Unknown which category applies — ask the Ministry.**
- **Packaged fruit drinks:** which category a small home producer falls under ("manufacturing, small scale" or "artisans/petty traders") is not stated. **Confirm with the Ministry.**
- Some figures in the Ministry's table look like printing errors. For example, a renewal fee is higher than the registration fee in two categories. Confirm any fee with the Ministry before paying.

### Where to apply (SP-OY)
- Room 50, Ministry of Trade, Industry, Investment and Cooperatives, Secretariat, Ibadan (Monday to Friday, 8am to 4pm)
- Phone: 08069033422. Email: trade@oyostate.gov.ng
- Online payment: payment.oyostatebir.com

## Requirement: Lagos State business premises registration
- **Requirement ID:** S-LA-01
- **Issued by:** Lagos State Internal Revenue Service (LIRS)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, retail pharmacy, furniture business (and an online cosmetics store with premises in Lagos — to confirm)
- **Applies in:** Lagos
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)
- **Must be done before:** carrying on business at the premises
- **Why it comes after CAC registration:** registration is done on the LIRS eTax portal using the business's CAC number. (SP-LA-2)

### The law (SP-LA-1)
Business premises registration in Lagos is governed by the Taxes and Levies (Approved List for Collection) Act 1998, as amended.

### Fees (SP-LA-1)
The Act sets these maximum fees:

| Location | Registration | Annual renewal |
|---|---|---|
| Urban area | up to ₦10,000 | up to ₦5,000 per year |
| Rural area | ₦2,000 | ₦1,000 per year |

Whether a business location is urban or rural is decided by the Lagos State Ministry of Finance. The classification can be checked on the Lagos State Government website. (SP-LA-1)

### Steps (SP-LA-2)
1. Create an account for the business on the LIRS eTax portal: https://etax.lirs.net
2. Select taxpayer type "Corporate".
3. Enter the CAC number and click Proceed.
4. Enter all the company details requested in the form and click Submit.
5. Go to Generate/Pay Bill. Then:
   - select the agency closest to the business
   - choose "Business Premises" as the revenue item
   - select the payment period
   - enter the amount
   - click Generate
6. Click Pay Now to pay the bill.
7. A Lagos State revenue receipt for business premises is sent to the email address given, and can also be downloaded from the portal.

### Documents needed (SP-LA-2)
The process only asks for the business's CAC number and company details. No other documents are listed.

### Where to apply
- LIRS eTax portal: https://etax.lirs.net
- LIRS website: https://lirs.gov.ng/

The other link provided for Lagos (igr.ng) belongs to the Kano State Internal Revenue Service, not Lagos, so it is not used.

## Requirement: FCT business premises levy
- **Requirement ID:** S-FCT-01
- **Issued by:** FCT Internal Revenue Service (FCT-IRS)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, retail pharmacy, furniture business (and an online cosmetics store with premises in Abuja — to confirm)
- **Applies in:** FCT
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01); federal Tax ID (R02)

### What the source says (SP-FCT, secondary)
The order for a new business in Abuja is:
1. Register with CAC.
2. Get a Tax ID from the national Tax ID portal.
3. If the business has staff, register with FCT-IRS as an employer, to remit PAYE monthly and file the annual employer return.
4. Register the premises for the business premises, shop or kiosk levy, plus any sector permits, such as a food and water handling permit (relevant to packaged fruit drinks).

FCT-IRS also collects environment, waste and signage charges on behalf of the FCT Administration.

### Enforcement (SP-FCT, secondary)
FCT-IRS enforces through court orders. It has sealed hotels, schools, restaurants and offices for failing to file or pay, after demand notices went unanswered. Unsealing premises without a court order is a criminal offence.

### Not stated in the source
Documents, fees and renewal rules for the business premises levy. **Unknown — contact FCT-IRS** (call centre 0700 220 0002; contactus@fctirs.gov.ng).

## Requirement: Rivers State business premises registration
- **Requirement ID:** S-RV-01
- **Issued by:** Rivers State Internal Revenue Service (RIRS)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, retail pharmacy, furniture business (and an online cosmetics store with premises in Rivers — to confirm)
- **Applies in:** Rivers
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01); Rivers State tax registration (RIVTIN) (R02-ST-RV)
- **Why it comes after RIVTIN:** business premises registration and renewal are paid on the RIRS taxpayer portal, RIVTAMIS, which needs a RIVTIN account. (SP-RV)

### Getting a RIVTIN (SP-RV)
1. Go to https://rivtamis.riversbirs.gov.ng/.
2. Open the Users menu and click Register.
3. Choose Corporate or Individual.
4. Search for your name or organisation. If it is found, update the details; if not, register.
5. Confirm and activate the account (activation takes about 24 hours).

Individuals verify with their NIN; companies with their CAC registration number. A valid email and phone number are needed, and a one-time code is sent by email and SMS.

### Business premises (SP-RV)
"Business Premises Registration/Renewal" is listed as a payment on the RIVTAMIS portal, by card or at a bank branch. The documents, fees and renewal deadline are not stated. **Unknown — contact RIRS.**

### Where to apply
- RIRS, Plot GS/1, Amadi Ama Layout, Port Harcourt. Phone: 08165019109, 09016221850. Email: info@riversbirs.gov.ng

## Requirement: Ogun State Business Premises Permit
- **Requirement ID:** S-OG-01
- **Issued by:** Ogun State Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment (MoITI)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, retail pharmacy, furniture business (and an online cosmetics store operating in Ogun — to confirm)
- **Applies in:** Ogun
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01). Informal businesses can also register using their proposed business name. (SP-OG-2)
- **Must be done before:** carrying on business at the premises

### Who must register (SP-OG-2)
All businesses operating in Ogun State, **except lock-up shops and kiosks**. The law is the Business Premises Registration Law (as amended in 2006). Section 26 sets offences and fines for not complying; the amounts are not stated in the source.

### Steps (SP-OG-2)
1. Create a free account at https://businesspermit.ogunstate.gov.ng.
2. Register the business and generate the invoice.
3. Pay online.
4. Print the certificate. It is issued immediately after successful registration.

### Fees (SP-OG-2)
The fee is set by the Business Premises Registration Law and calculated automatically. The portal generates the invoice at registration. The amounts are not published in the FAQ.

### Other rules (SP-OG-2)
- The certificate must be **displayed in a visible place** at the premises for government inspection.
- All branches of one business can be registered under one account. A warehouse at the same premises is covered by the same permit.
- Separate businesses need separate registrations, each with a different email address.

### Where to apply
- Online: https://businesspermit.ogunstate.gov.ng
- Email: businesspremisespermit@ogunstate.gov.ng. Phone: 08038536303, 08086169766
