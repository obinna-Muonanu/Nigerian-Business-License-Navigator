# Business registration with the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)

## Summary
The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) is Nigeria's federal registry for businesses. Registering with CAC is the first step for every business covered here: packaged fruit drinks, retail pharmacy, online cosmetics store and furniture business, in every state.

A business registers in one of four ways:

| Route | CAC registration type | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship | Business name | One owner trading under a business name |
| Partnership | Business name (with partners) | Two or more partners trading under a business name |
| Private limited company (Ltd) | Company limited by shares, private | A separate legal entity owned by one or more shareholders |
| Public limited company (Plc) | Company limited by shares, public | A larger company that can offer its shares to the public |

Only one route is needed. Whichever route is completed counts as CAC business registration.

## Comparing the four routes

| | Sole proprietorship | Partnership | Private limited company | Public limited company |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Legal status | Owner and business are the same person | Partners and business are the same persons | Separate legal entity | Separate legal entity |
| Liability | Owner personally liable for business debts | Partners personally liable | Shareholders' liability limited | Shareholders' liability limited |
| Lawyer needed | No (CAC-1) | No (CAC-1) | Yes, a legal practitioner signs the declaration of compliance (CAC-5) | Yes (CAC-5) |
| Minimum share capital | None | None | ₦100,000 issued share capital (CAC-8) | ₦2,000,000 issued share capital, at least 25% paid up (CAC-8, CAC-9) |
| Name reservation fee | ₦500 (CAC-3) | ₦500 (CAC-3) | ₦500 (CAC-3) | ₦500 (CAC-3) |
| Registration fee | ₦10,000 (CAC-3) | ₦10,000 (CAC-3) | From ₦10,000, by share capital (CAC-3) | From ₦20,000, by share capital (CAC-3) |
| Official processing time | 24 working hours (CAC-4) | 24 working hours (CAC-4) | 24 working hours (CAC-4) | 24 working hours (CAC-4) |
| Annual returns fee | ₦3,000 (CAC-3) | ₦3,000 (CAC-3) | ₦5,000 (CAC-3) | ₦10,000 (CAC-3) |

**Retail pharmacy rule:** a retail pharmacy must be wholly owned by a registered pharmacist, or by a partnership of registered pharmacists. A business name certificate is accepted for a pharmacist-owned retail pharmacy. If a company is used, the PCN application needs a certified copy of the company's Memorandum and Articles of Association. (CAC-7)

## Requirement: CAC business registration
- **Requirement ID:** R01
- **Issued by:** Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, retail pharmacy, online cosmetics store, furniture business
- **Applies in:** all states (federal requirement)
- **Satisfied by:** completing one of R01-SP (sole proprietorship), R01-PT (partnership), R01-LTD (private limited company) or R01-PLC (public limited company)
- **Must be done after:** nothing. This is the first step.
- **Must be done before:** federal tax registration with the Nigeria Revenue Service; NDPC data protection registration; NAFDAC facility and product registration; PCN pharmacy premises registration; SON product certification; state premises, signage and tax registrations; CAC annual returns.
- **Why it comes first:** the other regulators ask for proof that the business legally exists. This is the CAC certificate, registration number or certified extract. For companies, the CAC registration number also serves as the company's tax identification reference with the Nigeria Revenue Service.

## Requirement: Business name registration — sole proprietorship
- **Requirement ID:** R01-SP
- **Issued by:** Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
- **Applies to:** all four business types, when one person owns the business
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing
- **Satisfies:** CAC business registration (R01)

### Documents and information needed
- Two proposed business names, in order of preference
- Nature of the business
- Proprietor's full name, residential address (not a P.O. box), nationality, date of birth, gender, email address and phone number
- Valid means of identification: NIN is recommended; an international passport, driver's licence or voter's card is also accepted. Upload a colour scan.
- Passport photograph
- Signature on a plain white background
- Business address (a home address is acceptable if the business runs from home)

(CAC-5. CAC's own page only asks for the "relevant registration documents".)

### Steps
1. Search for name availability on the CAC website. (CAC-1)
2. Reserve the name. The reservation is valid for 60 days, so the registration must be completed within that time. (CAC-10)
3. Complete pre-registration form CAC-BN on the Company Registration Portal and upload the documents. (CAC-1)
4. Pay the registration fee online. (CAC-1)
5. Download the electronic Certificate of Registration and the Certified Extract of registration information. (CAC-1)

### Official fees (CAC-3)
- Name reservation: ₦500 per name (₦5,000 where restricted words are used)
- Registration, including certified true copies of the registration documents: ₦10,000
- Keeping the proprietor's residential address private (optional): ₦25,000

### Official timelines (CAC-4)
- Name availability search: 4 working hours
- Business name registration: 24 working hours
- Applications that start at a CAC state office: 7 working days

### Where to apply
- Company Registration Portal: https://icrp.cac.gov.ng/

## Requirement: Business name registration — partnership
- **Requirement ID:** R01-PT
- **Issued by:** Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
- **Applies to:** all four business types, when two or more partners own the business. A pharmacy partnership must be made up of registered pharmacists.
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing
- **Satisfies:** CAC business registration (R01)

### Documents and information needed
- Two proposed business names, in order of preference
- Nature of the business
- For **each partner**: full name, residential address (not a P.O. box), nationality, date of birth, gender, email address, phone number, valid means of identification (colour scan), passport photograph and signature
- Business address
- Partnership agreement: recommended, but not always required

(CAC-5)

### Steps
Same as sole proprietorship: name search, name reservation (valid 60 days), form CAC-BN with every partner's details, online payment, download of the certificate and extract. (CAC-1, CAC-10)

### Official fees (CAC-3)
- Name reservation: ₦500 per name
- Registration, including certified true copies of the registration documents: ₦10,000
- Keeping partners' residential addresses private (optional): ₦25,000

### Official timelines (CAC-4)
- Name availability search: 4 working hours
- Business name registration: 24 working hours

### Where to apply
- Company Registration Portal: https://icrp.cac.gov.ng/

## Requirement: Private limited company registration
- **Requirement ID:** R01-LTD
- **Issued by:** Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
- **Applies to:** all four business types, when the owners choose a private company
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing
- **Satisfies:** CAC business registration (R01)

### Documents and information needed
- Two proposed company names, in order of preference
- Registered head office address in Nigeria (not a P.O. box)
- Principal business activities
- Issued share capital (minimum ₦100,000) and how the shares are split between shareholders (CAC-5, CAC-8)
- **Directors (at least one):** full personal details, occupation, valid means of identification, passport photograph and signature. A company acting as a director must provide its certificate of incorporation and a board resolution. (CAC-5)
- **Shareholders (at least one):** the same personal details. A company acting as a shareholder must provide its certificate of incorporation and a board resolution. (CAC-5)
- **Company secretary:** under CAMA 2020, private companies no longer need one (CAC-9). One agency guide says a secretary is still required when share capital is ₦100 million or more, or when a director is a company (CAC-5). **Sources disagree — confirm on the CAC portal.**
- Memorandum and Articles of Association (MEMART)
- Statement of share capital and allotment
- Declaration of compliance signed by a legal practitioner

(CAC-5, CAC-8, CAC-9. CAC's own page only asks for "supporting documents".)

### Steps (CAC-2)
1. Search for name availability on the CAC website.
2. Reserve the name and proceed to registration. The reservation is valid for 60 days. (CAC-10)
3. Complete the digital pre-registration on the Company Registration Portal and upload the supporting documents.
4. Pay the filing fees and the statutory stamp duty through the portal's payment gateway.
5. Download the electronic Certificate of Incorporation and the Certified Extract.

### Official fees (CAC-3)
- Name reservation: ₦500 (₦5,000 for restricted words where applicable)
- Registration:
  - Issued share capital of ₦1 million or less: ₦10,000
  - Above ₦1 million up to ₦500 million: ₦5,000 for every ₦1 million or part of it
  - Above ₦500 million: ₦7,500 for every ₦1 million or part of it
- Optional same-day post-incorporation filing: ₦30,000 on top of the normal filing fees
- Stamp duty: payable, but the amount is not stated in CAC's fee summary. **Unknown — confirm on the CAC portal.**

### Official timelines (CAC-4)
- Name availability search: 4 working hours
- Company registration: 24 working hours
- Applications that start at a CAC state office: 7 working days

### Where to apply
- Company Registration Portal: https://icrp.cac.gov.ng/

## Requirement: Public limited company registration
- **Requirement ID:** R01-PLC
- **Issued by:** Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
- **Applies to:** all four business types, when the owners choose a public company
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing
- **Satisfies:** CAC business registration (R01)

### Documents and information needed
Everything required for a private limited company, plus:
- Issued share capital of at least ₦2,000,000, with at least 25% paid up (CAC-8, CAC-9)
- At least three independent directors (CAMA 2020, section 275). An independent director must not, in the previous two years, have been an employee or the auditor of the company, or have owned more than 30% of its shares. (CAC-8, CAC-9)
- A company secretary, which is mandatory for public companies (CAC-9)
- A register of persons with significant control (5% or more), kept from the start (CAC-9)

The minimum total number of directors for a public company is not stated in the sources read. **Unknown — confirm on the CAC portal.**

### Steps (CAC-2)
Same as a private limited company: name search, name reservation, digital pre-registration with supporting documents, payment of filing fees and stamp duty, and download of the certificate and extract.

### Official fees (CAC-3)
- Name reservation: ₦500
- Registration:
  - Issued share capital of ₦1 million or less: ₦20,000 (in practice a public company needs at least ₦2 million, so the next band applies)
  - Above ₦1 million up to ₦500 million: ₦10,000 for every ₦1 million or part of it. At the ₦2 million minimum, this is ₦20,000.
  - Above ₦500 million: ₦15,000 for every ₦1 million or part of it
- Stamp duty: payable; amount not stated in CAC's fee summary. **Unknown — confirm on the CAC portal.**

### Official timelines (CAC-4)
- Company registration: 24 working hours

### Where to apply
- Company Registration Portal: https://icrp.cac.gov.ng/

## Requirement: CAC annual returns
- **Requirement ID:** R01-AR
- **Issued by:** Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
- **Applies to:** every registered business name and company
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)
- **Why it comes after:** annual returns are filed against an existing CAC registration.
- **How often:** every year

### When to file
- **Business names (sole proprietorship, partnership):** every year between January and June, starting the year after registration. (CAC-6)
- **Companies (private and public):** within 42 days after the company's annual general meeting, together with audited accounts. Single-shareholder companies have different rules. (CAC-8)

### Official fees and late penalties (CAC-3)
| Route | Annual returns fee | Late filing: daily penalty | Late filing: one-off penalty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business name (sole proprietorship, partnership) | ₦3,000 | ₦150 | ₦5,000 |
| Small company | ₦5,000 | ₦250 | ₦5,000 |
| Private company (other than small) | ₦5,000 | ₦500 | ₦10,000 |
| Public company | ₦10,000 | ₦1,000 | ₦25,000 |

### What happens if returns are missed
Penalties build up for each missed year. CAC may require all outstanding years to be filed before the record is active again, and a business can eventually be marked inactive or removed from the register. (CAC-6)

### Official timeline (CAC-4)
- Annual returns are processed in 24 working hours.

### Where to file
- CAC post-registration portal: post.cac.gov.ng (CAC-6)

## Other official charges (CAC-3)
- Certified true copy of registration documents or extracts: ₦5,000 each

## What this document does not cover
- **Stamp duty amount** for company registration. Check the CAC portal.
- **Minimum number of directors for a public company.** Check the CAC portal.
- **Agency prices.** Registration agents advertise packages (for example ₦45,000 for a business name) that include their own fees. These are not CAC's official fees.
- **Other structures** (company limited by guarantee, incorporated trustees, limited partnership, limited liability partnership). These are not covered.
