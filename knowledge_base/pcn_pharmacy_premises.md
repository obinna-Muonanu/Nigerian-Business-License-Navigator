# Opening a retail (community) pharmacy

## Summary
A retail (community) pharmacy is a pharmaceutical premises that dispenses and sells medicines directly to the public. Operating a pharmacy without PCN registration is illegal. (PCN-6) Opening one needs four things from the Pharmacy Council of Nigeria (PCN):
1. A **superintendent pharmacist** with a current annual licence to practise.
2. A **business ownership structure** that meets PCN's rules.
3. **Location approval** for the proposed site.
4. **Premises registration**, after an inspection of the finished premises. It must be renewed every year.

Applications go to the PCN Registrar through the **PCN zonal or state office** where the pharmacy will operate. (PCN-1, PCN-2) An older secondary source says applications go through the State Director of Pharmaceutical Services (PCN-6). The current official guidelines name the PCN zonal or state office.

This document covers physical community pharmacies only. Online pharmacies are a separate PCN premises class (PCN-3) and are not covered.

## Requirement: Superintendent pharmacist registered and licensed
- **Requirement ID:** P01
- **Issued by:** Pharmacy Council of Nigeria (PCN)
- **Applies to:** retail pharmacy
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing
- **Must be done before:** location approval (P04); premises registration (P05)
- **Why it comes first:** the pharmacist's current annual licence is a required document for both location approval and premises registration. The pharmacy must be under the direct personal control of this pharmacist. (PCN-1, PCN-2)

### Who can be the superintendent pharmacist (PCN-1)
- A fully registered pharmacist who has completed NYSC or holds a certificate of exemption
- Up to date with the Mandatory Continuing Professional Development (MCPD) programme, where eligible
- Holds only one full-time job, confirmed by a signed undertaking
- Has all pharmaceutical business under their direct personal control and supervision, confirmed by an undertaking from the managing director

### Annual licence to practise (Form J) (PCN-1, PCN-3)
- The licence year runs from 1 January to 31 December.
- **Renewal steps:** pay the prescribed fee, buy a scratch card and apply online. Recommendation for approval takes 3 working days. The electronic licence is emailed, and a stamped hard copy follows within 7 working days.
- **Fees by years since registration (PCN-3):**

| Years since registration | Annual licence fee |
|---|---|
| NYSC | ₦2,500 |
| 9 years and below | ₦4,000 |
| 10 to 14 years | ₦7,000 |
| 15 to 39 years | ₦10,000 |
| 40 years and above | Free |

## Requirement: Business ownership meets PCN rules
- **Requirement ID:** P02
- **Issued by:** Pharmacy Council of Nigeria (PCN); business registered with CAC
- **Applies to:** retail pharmacy
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)
- **Must be done before:** location approval (P04)
- **Why it comes after CAC registration:** PCN asks for the CAC documents as evidence of ownership. (PCN-1)

### Rules by CAC route (PCN-1, PCN-6)
| CAC route | PCN rule | CAC documents PCN asks for |
|---|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship (business name) | Accepted for a pharmacist-owned community pharmacy | Business name registration certificate |
| Partnership (business name) | Accepted when the partners are registered pharmacists | Business name registration certificate |
| Private or public limited company | At least one pharmacist on the board of directors (PCN-1) | Certificate of incorporation; certified Memorandum and Articles of Association; certified CAC particulars of directors; the pharmacist director's current annual licence |

A secondary source states the stricter rule that a retail pharmacy must be wholly owned by a registered pharmacist, or by a partnership of registered pharmacists. (PCN-6) **Confirm with the PCN state office which rule applies.**

## Requirement: Premises meet PCN siting and structure standards
- **Requirement ID:** P03
- **Issued by:** Pharmacy Council of Nigeria (PCN)
- **Applies to:** retail pharmacy
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing (choose the site before applying)
- **Must be done before:** location approval (P04) for the site rules; pre-registration inspection (P05) for the structure and equipment rules
- **Why it comes first:** PCN inspects the site and building against these standards. (PCN-2)

### Where a pharmacy may be sited (PCN-2)
- **Not permitted:** motor parks, petrol stations, markets, clustered environments, kiosks, containers or stalls.
- **Distance between standalone pharmacies:** at least 200 metres. A standalone pharmacy must also be at least 200 metres from a pharmacy in a shopping centre.
- **Shopping centres:** no more than three pharmaceutical premises, not next door to each other, with at least five shops between them.
- **Patent medicine shops** must be at least 400 metres from a pharmacy.
- **How distances are measured:** from front main door to front main door, by the shortest accessible route.
- **Changing surroundings:** the premises must be in a sanitary environment. If the area later becomes clustered or a market grows around it, the pharmacy must relocate within two years.

### Building (PCN-2)
- Minimum floor area of **30 square metres**.
- Concrete walls; floors of concrete, tiles or terrazzo; ceiling at least **3.05 metres** high.
- Painted walls and well-arranged, painted shelves.
- Separate areas for dispensing, counselling and patient care, client waiting, and storage.
- An adequately furnished office for the superintendent pharmacist.
- Good lighting and ventilation, with **functional air conditioning and fans**.
- Fire extinguishers, a sand bucket and emergency exits.
- Premises and surroundings kept neat, with no bushes or litter.

### Equipment and references (PCN-2)
- A functional refrigerator used only for heat-sensitive (thermolabile) medicines
- Thermometers and hygrometers
- Pallets or shelves for storage
- Good water supply and a hand-washing basin or sink
- Metric balances and weights where compounding is done
- A secured poisons cupboard
- Current reference books, including the Nigerian Standard Treatment Guidelines, British Pharmacopoeia, British National Formulary, National Formulary and Essential Drugs List, a compilation of pharmacy laws, current gazettes of registered pharmacists and pharmacies, and the Compendium of Minimum Standards of Pharmacy Practice in Nigeria

## Requirement: Location approval
- **Requirement ID:** P04
- **Issued by:** Pharmacy Council of Nigeria (PCN), through its zonal or state office
- **Applies to:** retail pharmacy
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** superintendent pharmacist licensed (P01); ownership meets PCN rules (P02); site meets siting rules (P03)
- **Must be done before:** premises registration (P05)
- **Why it comes before premises registration:** PCN first checks that the proposed site and its size meet requirements. The pre-registration inspection takes place only after the location is approved. (PCN-2)

### Documents needed (PCN-2)
- Superintendent pharmacist's current annual licence, or renewal application
- Evidence of fee payment
- Superintendent pharmacist's appointment letter, where applicable
- Certified certificate of incorporation, or business name registration for a pharmacist-owned retail pharmacy
- Certified Memorandum and Articles of Association
- Particulars of directors
- Pharmacist director's current annual licence
- Signed disclaimer of liability form

### Timeline (PCN-2)
- Within two weeks of applying at the zonal or state office, if all conditions are met.

### Fees
- PCN's fee table includes a "location fee" column, but the figures for retail could not be read reliably from the scanned form. (PCN-3) **Unknown — confirm with the PCN state office.**

## Requirement: Premises registration (pre-registration inspection and Form B)
- **Requirement ID:** P05
- **Issued by:** Pharmacy Council of Nigeria (PCN) Registrar, through the zonal or state office
- **Applies to:** retail pharmacy
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** location approval (P04); premises built and equipped to standard (P03)
- **Must be done before:** opening the pharmacy to the public
- **Why it comes after location approval:** the pre-registration inspection is carried out only after the location is approved and Form B has been submitted. It checks that the building and facilities meet PCN standards. (PCN-2)

### Documents needed (PCN-1, PCN-2)
- Application letter to register the premises, addressed to the Registrar
- Completed Form B, obtained from the PCN zonal or state office (in duplicate, with a recent colour passport photograph and a list of enclosed documents) (PCN-3)
- Photocopy of the superintendent pharmacist's current annual licence (or Form J)
- Receipt for the inspection and registration fees (paid with a Remita payment number) (PCN-3)
- Resignation and acceptance letters from any previous employer, where applicable
- Appointment letter at the new premises, where applicable
- Legal agreement between the superintendent pharmacist and the employer, where applicable
- Certificate of incorporation, or business name registration for a pharmacist-owned pharmacy
- Certified Memorandum and Articles of Association (companies)
- Certified true copies of the particulars of directors from CAC (companies)
- NYSC certificate or certificate of exemption
- Superintendent pharmacist's undertaking to hold only one full-time job
- Managing director's undertaking that the superintendent pharmacist personally supervises the business
- Inter-state movement form, where applicable
- Pharmacist director's current annual licence

### What Form B asks for (PCN-3)
- Name and address of the premises, email and phone numbers
- The registered pharmacist in control and their registration number
- Whether the premises is new or old (for old premises, the last certificate number and date)
- Scope of practice (for this business: retail and dispensing)
- Category and class (for this business: community pharmacy)
- Directors' names, professions, registration numbers and annual licence numbers
- The pharmacist's residential address

The applicant must agree to tell the Registrar immediately about any change of premises address or of the pharmacist in charge.

### Steps and timelines
1. Get Form B from the PCN zonal or state office and complete it. (PCN-1)
2. Submit the application and documents to the Registrar through the zonal or state office where the pharmacy will operate. (PCN-1)
3. Tell PCN when the premises is ready. Inspection takes place within **two weeks** of that notice, and the inspection report goes to the Registrar within **two weeks** after the inspection. (PCN-2) The registration guideline says the inspection takes place within **one month** of receiving all documents and fees. (PCN-1)
4. After the Pharmaceutical Inspection Committee recommends the premises, the state or zonal office forwards the report within **10 working days**. (PCN-1)
5. The Registry issues the premises licence within **21 working days** if all requirements are met. (PCN-1)

### Fees
- PCN's fee table lists new registration, renewal, inspection and location fees for each premises category, including community pharmacy. (PCN-3) The retail figures could not be read reliably from the scanned form. **Unknown — confirm with the PCN state office.**
- A 2022 secondary source gives the inspection fee as ₦10,000 for each of the first two visits and ₦20,000 for each later visit. (PCN-6) Confirm before relying on it.

## Requirement: Display certificates and meet operating standards
- **Requirement ID:** P06
- **Issued by:** Pharmacy Council of Nigeria (PCN)
- **Applies to:** retail pharmacy
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** premises registration (P05)
- **What is required (PCN-2):**
  - Display the current premises registration certificate and the superintendent pharmacist's current annual licence conspicuously. Display the superintendent pharmacist's name.
  - Pharmacists wear white overalls with name tags; pharmacy technicians wear white overalls with blue collars and name tags.
  - Keep a poisons book recording all controlled substances received and dispensed, entered by the pharmacist on duty.
  - Only a licensed pharmacist handles controlled substances or poisons. Scheduled prescription medicines are locked away when the superintendent pharmacist is absent.
  - Keep records of purchases and sales, and patient medication profiles where applicable.
- **Signage:** only PCN-registered names may appear on signs. The Pharmaceutical Society of Nigeria emblem may only be used by registered pharmacist-owned premises. (PCN-6)
- **Routine inspections:** PCN inspects community pharmacies periodically, based on each pharmacy's risk rating from its last inspection. It also carries out investigative inspections after complaints. (PCN-2)

## Requirement: Annual renewal of premises registration
- **Requirement ID:** P07
- **Issued by:** Pharmacy Council of Nigeria (PCN)
- **Applies to:** retail pharmacy
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** premises registration (P05); superintendent pharmacist's annual licence renewed (P01)
- **When:** renewal is due from 1 January every year (PCN-1). Retention fees must be paid before 31 January. (PCN-3)
- **How (PCN-1):** apply online using a scratch card, the same process as the annual licence. Recommendation takes 3 working days. The electronic certificate is emailed, and a stamped hard copy follows within 7 working days.
- **Late renewal (PCN-6, secondary):** a pharmacy not renewed by 31 January pays the fee plus a 50% fine. A pharmacy not renewed by 31 March is closed. **Confirm with PCN.**
- **Fees:** **Unknown — confirm with the PCN state office** (the renewal column of PCN's fee table could not be read reliably).

## Changes after registration
Changes to the superintendent pharmacist, the premises name, structure or location, or the scope of practice need Form B, a letter explaining the change, evidence of it and any required legal documents, submitted to the PCN state office. Processing takes 14 working days. (PCN-1)

If the Registrar plans to suspend or revoke a licence, the holder has 14 days to give reasons. If those reasons are rejected, the holder can appeal to the Council within 14 days. (PCN-1)

## NAFDAC and retail pharmacies
NAFDAC's Good Manufacturing Practice Guidelines (PCN-5) are written for drug manufacturers. They state that they do not apply to compounding by a registered pharmacist. No separate NAFDAC licence for a retail pharmacy is described in the sources read. (PCN-5, PCN-6)

## What this document does not cover
- **Exact premises fees** (location, registration, inspection and renewal) for a community pharmacy. Confirm with the PCN state office.
- **Online pharmacies.** They are a separate PCN premises class.
- **Patent medicine vendor licences (PPMVL).** These are a different PCN licence for non-pharmacist medicine shops.
- **How a pharmacist first registers with PCN** (internship, provisional and full registration). The superintendent pharmacist is assumed to be already registered.
- **State permits** (business premises permit, signage). These are in the state permits document.
