# Tax registration — federal and state

## Summary
A new business deals with tax authorities at two levels.

- **Federal: Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS).** The NRS is the central authority for federal taxes. It replaced the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) under the 2025 tax reform, with effect from 1 January 2026. (NRS-1) It handles the national Tax ID, VAT, company income tax and the development levy.
- **State: the state internal revenue service** where the business operates. It handles personal income tax for self-employed people, including sole proprietors, and PAYE for employees. (ST-ED) It also collects state fees and levies, such as business premises registration fees. (ST-FCT)

Every business needs a Tax ID. Banks and other financial institutions must verify a Tax ID before opening or keeping a business account. (NRS-5)

## Requirement: Federal Tax ID
- **Requirement ID:** R02
- **Issued by:** Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS), jointly with the Joint Revenue Board (JRB) (NRS-2)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks, retail pharmacy, online cosmetics store, furniture business
- **Applies in:** all states (federal requirement)
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)
- **Must be done before:** VAT registration (R02-VAT); company income tax filing (R02-CIT); opening or keeping a business bank account (NRS-5)
- **Why it comes after CAC registration:** for companies, the Tax ID comes from the CAC registration number, so the business must be registered with CAC first. (NRS-2, NRS-4)

### What the Tax ID is
- A 13-digit number that replaces the old FIRS and JTB TINs. Existing TIN holders are expected to obtain an NRS Tax ID. (NRS-3)
- It is identity-based: it is derived from the National Identification Number (NIN) for individuals, and from the CAC registration number for businesses. (NRS-2)

### By route
| Route | What the Tax ID comes from | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| Sole proprietorship | The owner's NIN (NRS-2) | Retrieve on the Tax ID portal with NIN, first name, last name and date of birth (NRS-2) |
| Partnership | Each partner's NIN (NRS-2) | Same as above, for each partner |
| Private limited company | The company's CAC registration (RC) number (NRS-2, NRS-4) | Register on REV360; it checks company details against CAC records (NRS-4) |
| Public limited company | The company's CAC registration (RC) number (NRS-2, NRS-4) | Register on REV360 (NRS-4) |

Whether a business name (sole proprietorship or partnership) also receives a separate Tax ID from its BN number is not stated in the sources read. **Unknown — confirm on nrs.gov.ng.**

### Steps on the Tax ID portal (NRS-2)
1. Open the Tax ID portal at taxid.nrs.gov.ng.
2. Enter the NIN (individuals) or the CAC registration number (businesses).
3. Individuals add first name, last name and date of birth.
4. The portal checks the details against national tax and identity records.
5. If the records match and are active, the portal confirms the Tax ID. If they don't match, the details must be corrected with the relevant authority.

### REV360 (NRS-4)
- REV360 is the NRS's federal tax platform for registration, filing and payment. It launched in April 2026, replacing TaxPro-Max, at selfservice.nrs.gov.ng.
- Business Tax IDs are issued through REV360. It checks company details against CAC and NIMC records at sign-up, so any mismatch with the CAC record shows up immediately.
- One login covers the business's whole federal tax profile.

### Fees
No fee for obtaining a Tax ID is stated in the sources read. **Unknown — confirm on nrs.gov.ng.**

### Where to apply
- Tax ID portal: https://taxid.nrs.gov.ng/
- REV360: selfservice.nrs.gov.ng (NRS-4)

## Requirement: VAT registration
- **Requirement ID:** R02-VAT
- **Issued by:** Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS)
- **Applies to:** any of the four business types once it is no longer a small business (see below)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** Federal Tax ID (R02)
- **Why it comes after:** VAT registration is done against the business's Tax ID on REV360.

### Who must register (NRS-6)
- **Small businesses are exempt.** A business with annual turnover of ₦100 million or less, and total fixed assets of no more than ₦250 million, does not have to register for VAT, charge it, file VAT returns or remit it. Businesses providing professional services do not qualify for this exemption.
- A small business can choose to opt out of the exemption by written notice.
- Once a business goes above the threshold, VAT registration and compliance become mandatory.

### Rate
- 7.5% (NRS-6)

### Not stated in the sources read
- VAT return and payment deadlines, and the penalty for failing to register. **Unknown — confirm on nrs.gov.ng.**

## Requirement: Company income tax and development levy
- **Requirement ID:** R02-CIT
- **Issued by:** Nigeria Revenue Service (NRS)
- **Applies to:** private and public limited companies, in any of the four business types. Sole proprietors and partners pay personal income tax to the state instead (see the state sections).
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** Federal Tax ID (R02)
- **How often:** every year

### Small companies (NRS-5)
- A company with gross annual turnover of no more than ₦100 million and fixed assets below ₦250 million is a small company, unless it provides professional services.
- Small companies are exempt from company income tax, capital gains tax and the development levy.
- **They must still file a return to claim the exemption.** It is not granted automatically. (NRS-4)

### Other companies (NRS-5)
- Company income tax: 30%. This may be reduced to 25% by presidential order.
- Development levy: 4% of assessable profits. It replaces the old tertiary education tax, IT levy, NASENI levy and Police Trust Fund levy.

### Not stated in the sources read
- Filing deadlines and late-filing penalties for company income tax. **Unknown — confirm on nrs.gov.ng.**

## State tax registration
Each business also registers with the internal revenue service of the state where it operates.
- **Sole proprietors and partners** pay personal income tax on their business income to the state, through "direct assessment" for self-employed people. (ST-ED)
- **Every employer** deducts PAYE (pay-as-you-earn) tax from employees' pay and sends it to the state. (ST-ED, ST-FCT)
- States also collect other business fees and levies, including business premises registration fees. These are covered in the state permits document.

## Requirement: Oyo State tax registration
- **Requirement ID:** R02-ST-OY
- **Issued by:** Oyo State Internal Revenue Service (OYSIRS)
- **Applies to:** all four business types operating in Oyo State
- **Applies in:** Oyo
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)

### What the official site provides (ST-OY)
- The service operates under the Oyo State Revenue Administration Law of 18 August 2015 and the Personal Income Tax Act.
- Published regulations and procedures include:
  - the PAYE regulatory process
  - the Oyo State Assessment and Collection of Personal Income Tax Regulation 2021
  - the withholding tax payment process
  - the Oyo State Presumptive Tax Law, for small traders whose income is hard to assess
  - a December 2024 public notice that business-related revenue in Oyo is harmonised under a single demand notice
- Oyo enforces a tax clearance certificate as a condition for business transactions with state ministries, departments and agencies (notice of 1 December 2023).

### Not stated on the official site
- The taxpayer registration steps, documents and fees. **Unknown — contact OYSIRS.**

### Where to apply
- Office: Oyo State Internal Revenue Service Building, Oyo State Government Secretariat, Ibadan. Monday to Friday, 8am to 4pm.
- Email: info@oyostate.gov.ng

## Requirement: Lagos State tax registration
- **Requirement ID:** R02-ST-LA
- **Issued by:** Lagos State Internal Revenue Service (LIRS)
- **Applies to:** all four business types operating in Lagos State
- **Applies in:** Lagos
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)

### Registration (ST-LA-2)
- Registration and filing are done on the LIRS eTax portal, etax.lirs.net. It is the only approved filing platform, and paper filing has been phased out.
- To register as an individual: choose "Register as Taxpayer", select "Individual", enter BVN or NIN and date of birth, add address, occupation and nearest tax station, then submit. The Taxpayer ID is sent by email.
- Taxpayer IDs start with "N-" for individuals and "C-" for corporate taxpayers.
- Sole proprietors file under personal income tax.

### Annual returns (ST-LA-2)
- The statutory deadline is 31 March each year. (For 2026 it was extended once, to 14 April.)
- Late filing penalties under the Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025: ₦100,000 for the first month and ₦50,000 for each following month.

### Corporate registration (ST-LA-3)
A business registers on the eTax portal by choosing taxpayer type "Corporate", entering its CAC number and completing the company details form. The same account is used to pay the Lagos business premises fee (see the state permits document).

### Where to apply
- LIRS eTax portal: https://etax.lirs.net
- Website: https://lirs.gov.ng/

## Requirement: FCT tax registration
- **Requirement ID:** R02-ST-FCT
- **Issued by:** FCT Internal Revenue Service (FCT-IRS)
- **Applies to:** all four business types operating in the FCT (Abuja)
- **Applies in:** FCT
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)

### Registration (ST-FCT)
- Tax ID registration for individuals and businesses goes through the FCT-IRS tax portal, which links to the national registration forms.
- FCT-IRS states that its services are free of charge, and that processing fees or service charges are not allowed.
- Taxes are paid through Remita, Quickteller or eTranzact.

### Due dates (ST-FCT)
- PAYE: by the 10th of every month
- Employer's annual return: by 31 January
- Withholding tax: by 31 January

### Other revenue collected (ST-FCT)
FCT-IRS collects personal income tax, withholding tax, stamp duties, capital gains tax, business premises registration fees, development levy and market levies, among others. The rates are not given on the site.

### Where to apply
- Tax portal: https://taxporta.fctirs.gov.ng/login (create an account at https://taxporta.fctirs.gov.ng/create-account)
- Head office: 3 Abriba Close, Area 7, Garki, Abuja. Other offices: https://fctirs.gov.ng/locate-us/
- Call centre: 0700 220 0002. Email: contactus@fctirs.gov.ng

## Requirement: Edo State tax registration
- **Requirement ID:** R02-ST-ED
- **Issued by:** Edo State Internal Revenue Service (EIRS)
- **Applies to:** all four business types operating in Edo State
- **Applies in:** Edo
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)

### Registration (ST-ED)
- Register online through the EIRS e-registration service on the ERAS portal: https://eras.eirs.gov.ng/SignUp.aspx
- Returns are filed on ERAS, and a self-service portal is also available.

### Taxes that apply to a new business (ST-ED)
- **PAYE:** deducted from employees' pay (salaries, wages, bonuses, allowances and benefits in kind).
- **Direct assessment:** personal income tax for self-employed people, including traders and other business owners.
- **Withholding tax:** 5% on contracts, consultancy and commissions; 10% on rent, interest and dividends.
- **Edo development levy:** 3% of the contract sum. This is an Edo State levy, separate from the federal development levy.

### Not stated on the site
- Due dates and registration fees. **Unknown — contact EIRS.**

### Where to apply
- Tax offices in Edo's local government areas: https://www.eirs.gov.ng/back/tax-offices/
- Phone: 0813 097 0146. Email: info@eirs.gov.ng
