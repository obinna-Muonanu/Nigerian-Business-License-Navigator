# NAFDAC requirements for packaged fruit drinks made in Nigeria

## Summary
No packaged food or drink may be made, advertised, sold or distributed in Nigeria unless it is registered with NAFDAC. (NAF-3, NAF-6) A packaged fruit drinks business therefore needs two things from NAFDAC:
1. Its **production facility inspected and approved**.
2. **Each product registered**, which ends with a NAFDAC registration number on the label.

The facility requirements depend on the size of the business. NAFDAC has three categories:

| Category | Staff | Facility standard | Guideline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro scale | 1 to 5 (NAF-3) | At least one dedicated room; basic hygiene requirements | NAF-3 |
| Small scale | 6 to 9 (NAF-4) | At least two dedicated rooms; basic hygiene and manufacturing requirements | NAF-4 |
| Standard (medium/large) | Not limited | At least four rooms, fenced factory, treated water, full current good manufacturing practice (cGMP) | NAF-5 |

**Important for drinks: beverages are on NAFDAC's exempted list for micro and small scale.** NAFDAC's 2025 micro-scale and small-scale guidelines both list "beverages" among high-risk products that are exempted from those simpler categories. (NAF-3, NAF-4)
- **Small scale:** an exempted product can still be listed under small scale if the applicant has adequate technical skills and equipment. It then receives a listing certificate valid for two years. (NAF-4)
- **Micro scale:** the 2025 guideline gives no such exception for beverages. (NAF-3) NAFDAC's older micro-scale web page, dated January 2024, did not include beverages in its exempted list (NAF-1), so the two official sources disagree. **Confirm with NAFDAC whether a micro-scale drinks producer can be listed.**
- **Otherwise:** a drinks producer that does not qualify for micro or small scale must meet the standard pre-packaged food facility requirements (NAF-5).

## Requirement: Production facility meets NAFDAC requirements
- **Requirement ID:** D01
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC (Food Safety and Applied Nutrition Directorate)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)
- **Must be done before:** NAFDAC facility inspection (D03)
- **Why it comes first:** NAFDAC inspects the facility against these requirements. An unsatisfactory inspection results in a non-conformance notice, and the process stops. (NAF-5)

### Micro scale (NAF-3)
- **Premises:** a purpose-built structure, or part of an existing building, with one or more dedicated rooms. The production area needs its own entrance and exit, and must not open onto a toilet, bathroom or other source of contamination.
- **Pest control:** insect-proof screens on windows and doors; self-closing doors.
- **Environment:** good ventilation and lighting; kept clean at all times.
- **Storage:** raw materials and finished products on pallets or shelves.
- **Equipment:** food-contact parts made of food-grade, non-reactive material such as stainless steel; washed and dried before and after use.
- **Raw materials:** from traceable sources and of good quality.
- **Sanitation:** a hand-washing station; proper waste disposal; fumigation every three months; toilets away from production; no eating, drinking or smoking in the production area.
- **Staff:** basic education or relevant training; overalls, head covers, nose and mouth masks and gloves.
- **Records:** distribution records (distributor names and contacts, batch numbers, production and expiry dates) so products can be recalled.

### Small scale (NAF-4)
The same requirements as micro scale, except:
- **Premises:** at least two dedicated rooms, separated from living areas.
- **Quality control:** a sample of each finished product must be analysed by a public analyst, and the results documented.
- **Complaints and recalls:** every consumer complaint investigated and documented; NAFDAC notified of complaints, investigations and recalls.

### Standard pre-packaged food facility (NAF-5)
- **Premises:** at least four rooms (cloak room, packaging material store, production section and finished product store); a fence at least five feet high; not near a cemetery, abattoir, quarry or dump site; no wooden or prefabricated buildings.
- **Water:** from public mains, a spring or a borehole. A borehole must be at least 150 feet deep and at least 30 metres from any septic tank. Water must be treated by chlorination, sand and carbon filtration, micro-filtration and UV sterilisation.
- **Quality control:** separate production and quality control units. Comprehensive product analysis by a public analyst registered with the Institute of Public Analysts of Nigeria (IPAN). Shelf-life (stability) studies.
- **Staff:** technical officers with at least an OND; food handler medical tests at least twice a year.
- **Documents:** a process flow chart, a HACCP (food safety) plan, and training plans and records, in addition to the documents listed under D03.

## Requirement: Food handler medical tests for production staff
- **Requirement ID:** D02
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC requirement; tests done at a medical laboratory
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing
- **Must be done before:** NAFDAC facility inspection application (D03), because the medical certificate is one of the application documents
- **What is required (NAF-3, NAF-4):** a typhoid vaccination certificate (valid for three years), a Hepatitis A vaccination certificate, a stool test (microscopy, culture and sensitivity) and a Hepatitis A antigen test, following the National Guidelines for Food Handlers' Medical Test 2024.
- **Note:** the same guidelines' document checklist instead lists stool, sputum, Widal, Hepatitis B and urine tests. **The two lists differ — confirm with NAFDAC which tests to submit.**
- **How often:** at least once a year for micro scale (NAF-1); at least twice a year for standard facilities (NAF-5).

## Requirement: NAFDAC facility inspection application and inspection
- **Requirement ID:** D03
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC (Food Safety and Applied Nutrition Directorate)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01); federal Tax ID (R02); facility meets requirements (D01); food handler medical tests (D02)
- **Must be done before:** laboratory analysis of registration samples (D04); NAFDAC product registration (D05)
- **Why it comes after the Tax ID:** the NAFDAC portal asks for the business's tax identification number and validates it before the application can be saved. (NAF-3, NAF-4) The guidelines still say "TIN issued by FIRS"; since 1 January 2026 this is the NRS Tax ID.

### Documents needed (NAF-3, NAF-4)
- Application letter to the Director-General, NAFDAC (attention: the Director, Food Safety and Applied Nutrition Directorate), giving the exact physical address (not a P.O. box), email and phone numbers
- Evidence of business incorporation or business name registration (CAC)
- Evidence of trademark registration
- Coloured artwork of the product label (front, back, whole and side views)
- Company organogram with names, designations and qualifications of key personnel
- Appointment letters and acceptances for key personnel, and their credentials
- Standard operating procedures (SOPs) for production, quality control, cleaning, customer complaints, recall and distribution
- Proposed inspection date
- Food handler medical certificates for production staff (D02)
- List of raw materials and their sources
- Certificates of analysis for the finished product and for raw materials
- List of production equipment
- Personnel files (education and training certificates)
- Certificate of fumigation

Trademark registration is listed as required in the 2025 guidelines (NAF-3, NAF-4). NAFDAC's older micro-scale web page described it as "advisable" (NAF-1).

### Steps (NAF-3, NAF-4, NAF-5)
1. Log on to the NAFDAC product registration portal (NAPAMS), using an email address and password. The guidelines give its address as www.napams.org, but that address was not working on 2026-10-24. **Confirm the current portal address with NAFDAC.**
2. Enter and validate the business's tax identification number, then save.
3. Upload the documents above.
4. Generate the payment invoice on the portal and pay through Remita (www.remita.net) or at a commercial bank.
5. Submit a hard copy of the application letter and the payment receipt to the Food Safety and Applied Nutrition office in Isolo, Lagos, or the nearest NAFDAC office.
6. NAFDAC inspects the facility on the scheduled date.
   - **If unsatisfactory:** a non-conformance notice is issued and the process stops until it is fixed.
   - **If satisfactory:** NAFDAC takes registration samples for laboratory analysis and sends the inspection report on for registration.

### Fees
- **Tariff (NAF-7, 2019), facility inspection for food, renewable yearly:** micro scale ₦11,000; small scale ₦30,000; medium/large ₦40,000.
- **Micro-scale web page (NAF-1, January 2024):** production inspection ₦5,000 plus laboratory analysis ₦10,000, total ₦15,750 including 5% VAT.
- **These two official sources disagree.** The guidelines say the actual fee is generated on the portal invoice. (NAF-3, NAF-4)

### Timelines (NAF-4)
- Respond to any NAFDAC query within 15 working days, or the application is closed.
- The processing clock stops while a query or compliance request is outstanding.

### Where to apply
- NAFDAC product registration portal (NAPAMS). The address given in the guidelines, www.napams.org, was not working on 2026-10-24. **Confirm the current address with NAFDAC** (enquiries: 0700-1-NAFDAC).
- Food Safety and Applied Nutrition Directorate, Isolo, Lagos, or the nearest NAFDAC state office

## Requirement: Laboratory analysis of registration samples
- **Requirement ID:** D04
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC laboratory
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** satisfactory facility inspection (D03); product composition meets the Fruit Juice and Nectar Regulations (D08)
- **Must be done before:** NAFDAC product registration approval (D05)
- **Why it comes after the inspection:** registration samples are taken at the end of a satisfactory inspection. (NAF-3, NAF-4, NAF-5)
- **Fee:** ₦10,000 for micro scale, per NAFDAC's micro-scale web page (NAF-1). For other scales, the 2019 tariff's laboratory fees for general food could not be confirmed. **Unknown — confirm with NAFDAC.**

## Requirement: NAFDAC product registration
- **Requirement ID:** D05
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC (Food Registration and Regulatory Affairs Directorate)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks (one application per product)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01); satisfactory facility inspection (D03); laboratory analysis (D04); product composition meets the Fruit Juice and Nectar Regulations (D08)
- **Must be done before:** selling, distributing or advertising the product; labelling compliance (D06)
- **Why it comes after the inspection and laboratory analysis:** products go to NAFDAC's approval meeting only after satisfactory document review, GMP inspection and laboratory reports. (NAF-6)

### Documents needed (NAF-6)
- Application letter for product registration, on company letterhead, to the Director-General (attention: the Director, Food Registration and Regulatory Affairs Directorate)
- Evidence of business incorporation
- Batch formulation, where applicable
- Evidence of payment
- Contract manufacturing agreement, where applicable
- Evidence of brand name registration with the Trademarks Registry (Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment), in the owner's name
- Product labels or coloured artwork
- Comprehensive certificates of analysis for the product

### Steps (NAF-6)
1. Send the application letter.
2. Buy, complete and submit the online application form at https://registration.nafdac.gov.ng (a separate form for each product).
3. Submit the documents. After satisfactory review of documents, inspection and laboratory reports, the product goes to NAFDAC's product approval meeting.
4. Approved products receive a certificate of product registration. Products not approved receive compliance directives.

### Fees
- **Tariff (NAF-7, 2019), registration of locally made food:** micro enterprise ₦22,000; small ₦30,000; medium/large ₦40,000. Renewal costs the same as new registration.
- Paying the application fee does not mean the product is registered. (NAF-6)

### Timelines (NAF-6)
- Processing from submission to approval: 180 working days. (The guideline's text reads "one hundred and twenty (180)", so the figure is inconsistent. **Confirm with NAFDAC.**)
- Respond to compliance directives within 90 working days, or the application is closed.
- The clock stops while compliance directives are outstanding.

### Validity and renewal (NAF-6)
- Full registration is valid for 5 years.
- A listing (micro and small scale) is valid for 2 years.
- Renewal can be started 6 months before the certificate expires.
- NAFDAC can revoke, suspend or vary a certificate during its validity.

### Where to apply
- Online form: https://registration.nafdac.gov.ng
- Food Registration and Regulatory Affairs Directorate, Ground Floor, NAFDAC Office Complex, Isolo Industrial Estate, Isolo, Lagos. Email: food.registration@nafdac.gov.ng

## Requirement: Product composition meets the Fruit Juice and Nectar Regulations
- **Requirement ID:** D08
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC (Fruit Juice and Nectar Regulations 2021)
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks that are fruit juices, reconstituted juices, fruit nectars or fruit purees
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing (it is part of developing the product)
- **Must be done before:** laboratory analysis of registration samples (D04) and NAFDAC product registration (D05)
- **Why it comes first:** the product is tested against these composition, additive and contaminant limits during laboratory analysis, so the recipe must meet them before samples are taken.

### What the regulations cover (NAF-8)
They apply to fruit juice and nectar products made, imported, exported, advertised, sold, distributed or used in Nigeria (Regulation 1). They replaced the Fruit Juice and Nectar Regulations 2005 (Regulation 13). NAFDAC enforces them (Regulation 15).

### Define the product correctly (NAF-8)
The producer must first establish what the product legally is: a fruit juice, a reconstituted juice (made from concentrate), a fruit nectar, a puree or another fruit-based drink. Definitions and composition rules for each are in the First Schedule (Regulation 3).

### Composition and quality (NAF-8)
- **Registration:** fruit juice and nectar products must be registered with NAFDAC before they are made or sold (Regulation 2).
- **Taste and appearance:** the product must have the characteristic colour, aroma and flavour of the fruit juice (Regulation 4).
- **Soluble solids (°Brix):** juices, reconstituted juices and purees must meet minimum Brix levels (Regulation 7 and the Second Schedule). Brix measures soluble solids and is commonly used as an indicator of sugar concentration. For example, orange juice not from concentrate needs a minimum of 10 °Brix, and reconstituted orange juice from concentrate needs 11.2 (First Schedule).
- **Fruit-specific rules:** the First Schedule sets rules for individual juices, including orange, pineapple, grape, grapefruit, apple, blackcurrant and mixed fruit juices. These include minimum soluble solids, conditions for reconstituting concentrate with potable water, limits on added sugar, and limits on ethanol, essential oils and volatile acids.
- **Minimum fruit content in nectars (Third Schedule):**

| Fruit nectar | Minimum juice and/or puree content |
|---|---|
| Orange | 50% |
| Apple | 50% |
| Peach | 40% |
| Mango (pulpy) | 30% |
| Guava | 25% |
| Banana | 25% |

The minimum differs by fruit, so the figure for the specific fruit must be checked.

### Sweeteners and additives (NAF-8)
- Non-nutritive (artificial) sweeteners may not be used unless NAFDAC approves them (Regulation 2).
- Food additives and their maximum levels must follow NAFDAC approval and the Fourth Schedule (Regulation 5). Permitted categories include antioxidants (such as ascorbic acid), acidity regulators (such as citric and malic acid), carbonating agents, stabilisers (such as pectins), sweeteners for nectars, and processing aids for clarification and filtration. Being listed in the schedule does not on its own make a substance permitted; NAFDAC approval still applies.

### Maximum contaminant limits (NAF-8, Fifth Schedule)
| Contaminant | Maximum limit |
|---|---|
| Arsenic | 0.2 mg/kg |
| Lead | 0.1 mg/kg |
| Tin | 200 mg/kg |
| Copper | 5 mg/kg |
| Zinc | 5 mg/kg |
| Iron | 15 mg/kg |
| Copper, zinc and iron combined | 20 mg/kg |
| Insoluble mineral impurities (clarified juices) | 25 mg/kg |
| Patulin (apple juice and apple-juice ingredients) | 50 µg/kg (verify the unit against the original schedule) |

### Penalties (NAF-8, Regulations 11 and 12)
- **Individuals:** up to one year in prison, a fine of up to ₦800,000, or both.
- **Companies:** a fine of up to ₦5,000,000.
- Directors, managers, partners and other responsible officers can also be prosecuted.
- After a conviction, proceeds of the offence and property used to commit it can be forfeited to the Federal Government.

## Requirement: Product labelling
- **Requirement ID:** D06
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** NAFDAC product registration (D05), because the NAFDAC registration number must appear on the label
- **What the label must show (NAF-1, NAF-3, NAF-4)**, in English:
  - Ingredients (composition)
  - Net volume
  - Manufacturer's name and address
  - Batch number
  - Production date and best-before date
  - Storage conditions
  - Directions for use, where needed
  - NAFDAC registration number
- Nutritional claims must be backed by evidence.
- Labels must follow the Pre-packaged Food (Labelling) Regulations 2022. (NAF-6)
- **Extra rules for fruit juices and nectars (NAF-8, Regulations 8 to 10):**
  - Declare the fruit content.
  - Use a product name that accurately describes the product. Use the established name where one is approved; give an unfamiliar product a suitable descriptive name; follow the specified rules for naming blends and for claims about concentrates.
  - List all ingredients in descending order of proportion.

## Requirement: Renewal of product registration
- **Requirement ID:** D07
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** packaged fruit drinks
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** NAFDAC product registration (D05)
- **When:** before the certificate expires. Renewal can start 6 months before expiry (NAF-6). The facility inspection is renewable yearly (NAF-7).
- **Documents (NAF-1, NAF-5):** renewal letter to the Director-General; the expired product licence; the approved product label; receipt of payment for the renewal inspection and laboratory analysis; a GMP re-assessment request letter (standard facilities).

## Related: advertising
Registering a product does not give permission to advertise it. Advertising needs a separate NAFDAC application and approval. (NAF-6)

## What this document does not cover
- **Whether zobo (hibiscus drink) counts as a fruit juice or nectar under the Fruit Juice and Nectar Regulations 2021.** The regulations' schedules cover named fruits such as orange, pineapple, apple and mango. Zobo is not named in the summary used here. **Unknown — ask NAFDAC how zobo is classified.**
- **The full fruit-by-fruit Brix, added-sugar and additive limits** in the First, Second and Fourth Schedules. Only examples are included here. Check the full regulation for the specific fruit.
- **Whether a micro-scale (1–5 staff) drinks producer can be listed**, given that the 2025 guideline exempts beverages. Contact NAFDAC.
- **Current fees.** The 2019 tariff and the 2024 web page give different figures. Use the portal invoice or ask NAFDAC.
- **The trademark registration process.** It is listed as a NAFDAC document but is handled by the Trademarks Registry and is not covered here.
- **Packaged water.** It has separate NAFDAC guidelines and is not covered here.
