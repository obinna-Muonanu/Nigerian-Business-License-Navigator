# NAFDAC requirements for cosmetics

## Summary
No cosmetic may be manufactured, imported, exported, advertised, sold, distributed or used in Nigeria unless it is registered with NAFDAC. (COS-1, COS-2)

An online cosmetics store can get its products in three ways, and NAFDAC's requirements differ for each:

| Variant | What the business does | What NAFDAC requires |
|---|---|---|
| Resell | Sells other brands' products | Every product sold must already be NAFDAC-registered (see C10) |
| Import own brand | Has its own brand made abroad and imports it | Registers each product as an imported cosmetic (C20 to C25) |
| Make locally | Manufactures its own products in Nigeria | Registers each product as a cosmetic made in Nigeria, with a facility inspection (C30 to C35) |

A business can do more than one. The requirements for each variant then apply to the products sourced that way.

Requirements that apply to every variant (labelling, banned ingredients, advertising) are in the common section at the end.

---

## Variant: Resell registered brands

## Requirement: Sell only NAFDAC-registered cosmetics
- **Requirement ID:** C10
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (resell variant)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)
- **What it means:** cosmetics may not be sold or distributed in Nigeria unless they are registered with NAFDAC (COS-1, COS-2). A reseller must therefore stock only products that carry a NAFDAC registration number, which must appear on the label (COS-3).
- **Advertising:** no regulated product may be advertised in Nigeria unless it is registered. NAFDAC's approval must be sought before filming any advert, including adverts for social media. (COS-3)
- **Not stated in the sources:** whether a reseller needs its own NAFDAC registration or permit, and how to verify a product's NAFDAC number. **Unknown — ask NAFDAC.**

## Variant: Import own brand

## Requirement: Confirm the product is not on an import prohibition list
- **Requirement ID:** C20
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC / Federal Government
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (import own brand variant)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing
- **Must be done before:** imported cosmetics registration application (C21)
- **Why it comes first:** NAFDAC does not accept new registration applications for imported products on the Federal Government Import Prohibition List or the NAFDAC Ceiling List. (COS-1)

## Requirement: Imported cosmetics registration application
- **Requirement ID:** C21
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC (Drug Registration and Regulatory Affairs Directorate)
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (import own brand variant); one application per product
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01); trademark registration of the brand (T05); product not on a prohibition list (C20)
- **Must be done before:** electronic import permit and laboratory analysis (C22)
- **Why it comes after CAC and trademark registration:** evidence of both is a required application document. (COS-1)

### Documents needed (COS-1)
- **Application letter** to the Director-General, NAFDAC (attention: the Director, DR&R Directorate).
- **Evidence of business incorporation** from the Corporate Affairs Commission.
- **Notarised declaration** (Appendix 1 of the guideline), typed, signed by the declarant and notarised by a Nigerian notary public.
- **Power of attorney** from the foreign manufacturer, where the applicant acts for that manufacturer. It must:
  - be signed by the manufacturer's managing director, general manager, chairman or president
  - name the products and state the authority to register them with NAFDAC
  - state who owns the brand name or trademark
  - be notarised in the country of manufacture
  - be valid for at least five years
- **Contract manufacturing agreement**, where the applicant owns the brand or trademark and a foreign company makes the products. **This is the usual case for an own brand.** It must be signed by both parties, give their names and designations, list all products and be notarised in the country of manufacture.
- **Trademark registration evidence** from the Trademarks Registry (Federal Ministry of Industry, Trade and Investment), in the trademark owner's name.
- **Certificate of Manufacture and Free Sale.** It must:
  - be issued by the health or regulatory authority in the country of manufacture
  - show that the manufacturer is licensed and the product can be legally sold there
  - show the manufacturer's name and address and the product name
  - be authenticated by the Nigerian Embassy or High Commission in that country (or, where there is none, by a Commonwealth or ECOWAS country's mission)
- **Product labels or artwork.**
- **Letter of invitation for a GMP inspection**, written by the manufacturer. It must give the factory address (not the administrative office), email, phone and overseas contact person; the local agent's company name, address, phone, email and Nigerian contact person; and the list of products submitted.
- **Certificate of analysis** on the letterhead of the laboratory that tested the product. It must include all relevant technical parameters, the brand name, batch number, manufacturing and expiry dates, and the analyst's name, designation and signature.

Documents are uploaded to the portal. Originals must be shown on request. (COS-1)

### Where to apply (COS-1)
- NAFDAC registration portal (NAPAMS): https://registration.nafdac.gov.ng, following the NAPAMS User Manual
- Address the letter to: Director-General (NAFDAC), Attn: The Director, DR&R Directorate, Ground Floor, NAFDAC Office Complex, Isolo Industrial Estate, Apapa-Oshodi Expressway, Isolo, Lagos

## Requirement: Electronic import permit and laboratory analysis
- **Requirement ID:** C22
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (import own brand variant)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** imported cosmetics registration application (C21)
- **Must be done before:** registration approval (C24)
- **Why it comes after the application:** NAFDAC issues the electronic import permit only after screening the documents. The products can then be brought in and submitted for laboratory analysis. (COS-1)
- **What to submit with the laboratory samples (COS-1):** a letter of submission for analysis, the receipt of payment and the certificate of analysis.

## Requirement: GMP inspection of the foreign manufacturer
- **Requirement ID:** C23
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (import own brand variant)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** imported cosmetics registration application (C21), which includes the manufacturer's letter of invitation for the inspection
- **Must be done before:** registration approval (C24)
- **Note:** products go for approval after documentation review, GMP inspection and laboratory analysis, "where applicable". (COS-1) The guideline does not say when a foreign GMP inspection is waived. **Unknown — ask NAFDAC.**

## Requirement: Registration approval and certificate
- **Requirement ID:** C24
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC (Food and Drug Registration Committee)
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (import own brand variant)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** laboratory analysis (C22); GMP inspection where applicable (C23)
- **Must be done before:** selling, distributing or advertising the product
- **What happens (COS-1):** the product goes to the Food and Drug Registration Committee (FDRC). Approved products receive an electronic Certificate of Product Registration.

### Timelines (COS-1)
- 120 working days from acceptance of the submission to issue of the registration number.
- The clock stops while NAFDAC's compliance queries are outstanding.
- Queries must be answered within 90 working days, or the application is closed automatically.

### Validity (COS-1)
- A registration certificate is valid for five years.
- NAFDAC can revoke, suspend or vary it during that time.

### Fees (COS-1)
- Fees are set in the NAFDAC tariff. The guideline gives no amounts. **Unknown — check the tariff or ask NAFDAC.**
- Filing an application or paying the fee does not mean the product is registered.

## Requirement: Renewal of imported cosmetics registration
- **Requirement ID:** C25
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (import own brand variant)
- **Must be done after:** registration approval (C24)
- **When:** before the five-year certificate expires
- **How:** the guideline does not describe a renewal procedure. **Unknown — ask NAFDAC.**

## Variant: Make locally

There are two routes for cosmetics made in Nigeria:
- **MSME route (micro and small scale).** Simpler documents and lower fees, for small producers of simple products. (COS-3)
- **Standard route.** Full registration of cosmetics made in Nigeria, with a GMP inspection of the factory. (COS-2)

### Who counts as micro or small (COS-3)
NAFDAC's MSME page gives different figures in different places:
- **General definition:** micro 1 to 9 staff; small 10 to 49; medium 50 to 199.
- **Cosmetics FAQ:** a micro cosmetics facility has no more than five employees; a small facility has 1 to 10.
- **Number of products:** a micro applicant can register 1 to 5 products; more than five makes the applicant small scale.

**These figures conflict — confirm the current limits with NAFDAC.**

### Products allowed at micro scale (COS-3)
Petroleum jelly, shea butter, oil-based hair cream, oils (such as coconut oil), laundry detergents, starch, liquid air freshener and soaps.

## Requirement: Production facility and staff meet NAFDAC requirements
- **Requirement ID:** C30
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (make locally variant)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01)
- **Must be done before:** registration application (C31 or C32)
- **What NAFDAC recommends and requires (COS-3):**
  - Use distilled, de-ionised or purified water.
  - Use stainless steel equipment rather than plastic.
  - Buy chemicals only from NAFDAC-listed chemical marketers. The list is available from NAFDAC's Chemical Evaluation Directorate in Isolo, Lagos.
  - Production staff must have a medical certificate of fitness. For cosmetics this is a medical fitness certificate, not the food handler test.
  - Have the facility fumigated and keep the certificate.
- **Detailed facility requirements** for the standard route are published by NAFDAC's Drug Evaluation and Research (DER) Directorate (COS-2). They are not covered in the sources read. **Unknown — see the DER section of the NAFDAC website.**

## Requirement: MSME (micro or small scale) cosmetics registration
- **Requirement ID:** C31
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (make locally variant), for micro or small producers
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01); facility and staff requirements (C30)
- **Must be done before:** selling, distributing or advertising the product
- **Why it comes after CAC registration:** CAC registration is mandatory, because it shows the business is a legal entity on the public register. (COS-3)

### Documents for micro scale (COS-3)
- CAC registration (business name or company)
- Site use agreement, where applicable
- Trademark evidence (advised but optional at micro scale)
- One vetting sample and three labels
- Fumigation certificate
- Receipt of payment for production registration
- Medical fitness certificate for production staff, covering sputum, stool, urine, Widal and Hepatitis B tests

### Documents for small scale (COS-3)
- Application letter
- Standard operating procedures (SOPs) for production, distribution and recall, quality assurance, equipment cleaning, receiving packaging materials, personal hygiene, water analysis (where applicable) and environmental cleaning
- Medical fitness certificate for production staff (same tests as micro scale)
- List of raw materials and their sources
- Certificates of analysis for raw materials and finished products
- Certificate of fumigation
- Label and packaging material
- Soft copies of all documents. Each uploaded file must be no larger than 500 KB.
- Batch formulation: the list of all components and raw materials used. (Batch formulation is listed among NAFDAC's common MSME requirements.)

### Fees (COS-3)
- Micro-scale cosmetic registration: ₦41,000.
- Renewals get a 50% discount on the original 2020 tariff.
- Re-analysis of a failed product is charged laboratory fees at the original 2020 tariff.
- A 2020 "palliative" zero tariff for the first 200 new applicants was time-limited and has ended (the page gives its length as both three and six months from 15 May 2020).

### Timeline and validity (COS-3)
- Registration takes 90 working days.
- A listing certificate is valid for 2 years.
- An administrative waiver of 3 months applies to late renewals.

## Requirement: Standard registration of cosmetics made in Nigeria
- **Requirement ID:** C32
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC (Drug Registration and Regulatory Affairs Directorate)
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (make locally variant), for producers outside the MSME route; one application per product
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01); trademark registration of the brand (T05); facility requirements (C30)
- **Must be done before:** GMP inspection of the factory (C33)

### Documents needed (COS-2)
- Application letter to the Director-General, NAFDAC (attention: the Director, DR&R Directorate)
- Evidence of business incorporation from the Corporate Affairs Commission
- Contract manufacturing agreement, where applicable
- Evidence of brand name registration with the Trademarks Registry, in the trademark owner's name
- Product labels or artwork
- Comprehensive certificate of analysis on the testing laboratory's letterhead, covering all relevant technical parameters and showing the brand name, batch number, manufacturing and expiry dates, and the analyst's name, designation and signature

Documents are uploaded to the portal. Originals must be shown on request. (COS-2)

### Where to apply (COS-2)
- NAFDAC registration portal (NAPAMS): https://registration.nafdac.gov.ng

## Requirement: GMP inspection of the local factory and laboratory analysis
- **Requirement ID:** C33
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (make locally variant, standard route)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** standard registration application (C32)
- **Must be done before:** registration approval (C34)
- **Why it comes after the application:** NAFDAC inspects the production facility after a satisfactory review of the documents, and analyses the product in its laboratory where applicable. (COS-2)

## Requirement: Registration approval and certificate (made in Nigeria)
- **Requirement ID:** C34
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC (Food and Drug Registration Committee)
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (make locally variant, standard route)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** GMP inspection and laboratory analysis (C33)
- **Must be done before:** selling, distributing or advertising the product
- **What happens (COS-2):** the product goes to the Food and Drug Registration Committee's approval meeting. Approved products receive an electronic Certificate of Product Registration.

### Timelines (COS-2)
- 120 working days from acceptance of the submission to issue of the registration number.
- The clock stops while compliance queries are outstanding.
- Queries must be answered within 90 working days, or the application is closed automatically.

### Validity (COS-2)
- Five years or two years, depending on the case.
- NAFDAC can revoke, suspend or vary the certificate during that time.

### Fees (COS-2)
- Set in the NAFDAC tariff; the guideline gives no amounts. **Unknown — check the tariff or ask NAFDAC.**
- Filing an application or paying the fee does not mean the product is registered.

## Requirement: Renewal of locally made cosmetics registration
- **Requirement ID:** C35
- **Issued by:** NAFDAC
- **Applies to:** online cosmetics store (make locally variant)
- **Must be done after:** registration approval (C31 or C34)
- **When:** before the certificate expires (two years for a listing; five or two years for standard registration)
- **Fees:** MSME renewals get a 50% discount on the original 2020 tariff (COS-3).
- **How:** the guideline does not describe a renewal procedure. **Unknown — ask NAFDAC.**

## Rules for all variants

### Labelling (COS-1, COS-2, COS-3)
Labels must be informative and accurate, and follow the Cosmetics Products Labelling Regulations. They must show:
- Brand name (where applicable)
- Product identity
- Batch number
- Net content
- Manufacturing and expiry dates
- Manufacturer's name
- Country of origin
- NAFDAC registration number
- Storage conditions
- Ingredient list

Changes to an approved label must be approved by NAFDAC before they are made. (COS-3)

### Banned or restricted ingredients (COS-3)
- Lanolin in baby lotion
- Boric acid in baby powder
- Hydroquinone above 2%
- Mercury and its salts
- Corticosteroids in body creams
- Bithionol
- Vinyl chloride
- Methanol
- Halogenated salicylanilides
- Zirconium complexes in aerosols
- Chloroform
- Methylene chloride
- CFC propellants
- Hexachlorophene

### Product claims (COS-3)
- Claims must be backed by evidence. Simple claims such as "contains vitamin E" may not need substantiation at micro scale.
- A cosmetic can be called organic only if it contains 70% agricultural ingredients.

### Advertising, including online (COS-1, COS-2, COS-3)
- Registering a product does not give permission to advertise it. Advertising needs a separate NAFDAC application and approval.
- Approval must be obtained before filming any advert, including adverts for social media. This matters for an online store.

### Other rules (COS-3)
- Relocating a factory requires notifying NAFDAC and getting its authorisation first.
- NAFDAC does not encourage the use of consultants for registration; using one is at the applicant's own risk.

### What registration agencies say (COS-5, secondary)
An agency guide adds the following. These are not from NAFDAC, so confirm them before relying on them:
- The registration dossier usually includes the full ingredient composition with INCI names, the manufacturing method, specifications, stability data, safety data (MSDS) and product samples.
- Cosmetics trademarks are registered in Class 3 at the Trademarks Registry. Registration takes several months, including about two months of publication for opposition.
- For local manufacture, NAFDAC may ask for proof of premises ownership or lease, staff qualifications, SOPs and waste disposal methods. For imports, a GMP certificate from the country of origin may also be asked for.
