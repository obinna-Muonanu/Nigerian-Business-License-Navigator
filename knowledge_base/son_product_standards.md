# Product certification for locally made products (SON MANCAP)

## Summary
The Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON) was established under the SON Act No. 14 of 2015. (SON-3) It runs the **Mandatory Conformity Assessment Programme (MANCAP)**, a product certification scheme for **locally manufactured products**. (SON-2) MANCAP checks that products made in Nigeria meet the relevant Nigerian Industrial Standard (NIS) before they are sold or exported. Certified products carry a MANCAP certificate and a numbered MANCAP logo. (SON-1)

### Which of our business types it may apply to
- **Furniture business that makes its own furniture.** It is a locally manufactured product, so MANCAP is the relevant SON scheme. The sources read do not say which MANCAP sector covers furniture, or whether a furniture standard exists. **Confirm with the SON state office.**
- **A furniture business that only resells furniture made by others** does not manufacture, so MANCAP would not apply to it directly.
- **Packaged fruit drinks and cosmetics made locally.** "Food products" and "chemical products" are both listed among MANCAP's sectors (SON-1). The sources read do not say whether products already registered with NAFDAC also need MANCAP. **Unknown — confirm with SON and NAFDAC.**
- **Retail pharmacy and resold or imported cosmetics.** These do not involve local manufacture, so MANCAP does not apply. (Imported goods fall under SON's separate import scheme, SONCAP, which is not covered here. SON-3)

### MANCAP product sectors (SON-1)
- Textiles and leather
- Electrical and electronics
- Chemical products
- Mechanical products
- Civil, building and mining
- Food products

## Requirement: Confirm the product needs MANCAP and its Nigerian Industrial Standard
- **Requirement ID:** S01
- **Issued by:** Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)
- **Applies to:** furniture business (makes its own furniture); packaged fruit drinks and cosmetics made locally (to be confirmed)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** nothing
- **Must be done before:** MANCAP application (S02)
- **Why it comes first:** MANCAP certifies products against the relevant Nigerian Industrial Standard, so the business needs to know which standard applies. (SON-1, SON-2)
- **How:** contact the nearest SON state office for an enquiry. (SON-2) Nigerian standards are listed at https://son.gov.ng/standards/. (SON-3)

## Requirement: MANCAP application
- **Requirement ID:** S02
- **Issued by:** Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)
- **Applies to:** furniture business (makes its own furniture); packaged fruit drinks and cosmetics made locally (to be confirmed)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** CAC business registration (R01); product and standard confirmed (S01)
- **Must be done before:** factory inspections and product testing (S03)
- **Why it comes after CAC registration:** the CAC certificate of incorporation is a required document. (SON-2)

### Documents needed (SON-2)
- Certificate of incorporation from the Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC)
- On-the-spot test report. If that test is not comprehensive, a laboratory report from SON or from a laboratory accredited by the Institute of Public Analysts of Nigeria (IPAN) is also needed.
- Proof of payment for the MANCAP application form and for certification

The FAQ asks for a certificate of incorporation. Whether a business name (sole proprietorship or partnership) is accepted is not stated. **Unknown — confirm with SON.**

### How to apply (SON-1, SON-2)
- Contact the nearest SON state office for an enquiry and to get the application form.
- Online applications: https://mancap.son.gov.ng/

### Fees (SON-1, SON-2)
- Fees and inspection charges are published at SON state offices nationwide. SON's MANCAP page also links to a "MANCAP administrative charges" document. Neither gave amounts in the sources read. **Unknown — confirm at the SON state office.**

## Requirement: Factory inspections and product conformity
- **Requirement ID:** S03
- **Issued by:** Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)
- **Applies to:** furniture business (makes its own furniture); packaged fruit drinks and cosmetics made locally (to be confirmed)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** MANCAP application (S02)
- **Must be done before:** MANCAP certificate and logo (S04)
- **What is required (SON-2):**
  - At least two quarterly inspections of the factory.
  - The products must meet all requirements of the relevant Nigerian Industrial Standard.

## Requirement: MANCAP certificate and logo
- **Requirement ID:** S04
- **Issued by:** Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)
- **Applies to:** furniture business (makes its own furniture); packaged fruit drinks and cosmetics made locally (to be confirmed)
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** factory inspections and product conformity (S03)
- **Must be done before:** selling or exporting the product as MANCAP-certified

### Details (SON-1, SON-2)
- **Timeline:** 60 days from when the MANCAP Division office receives the application, if all requirements are met.
- **What is issued:** a MANCAP certificate and a MANCAP logo with a unique identification number. The logo can be fixed to each unit or printed on the label.
- **Validity:** three years.
- **During validity:** the SON state office for the area inspects the factory every quarter.
- **Export:** MANCAP-certified products can be exported to any West African country.

## Requirement: MANCAP recertification
- **Requirement ID:** S05
- **Issued by:** Standards Organisation of Nigeria (SON)
- **Applies to:** any business holding a MANCAP certificate
- **Applies in:** all states
- **Must be done after:** MANCAP certificate and logo (S04)
- **When:** after three years, when the certificate expires
- **What happens (SON-2):** the certification is renewed (recertified or revalidated) and a new certificate is issued.

## Option for small businesses: MANCAP Fast Track
- MANCAP Fast Track is a verification exercise, not a full certification. It checks a firm's production and quality assurance capability. (SON-2)
- A firm that passes receives a numbered MANCAP logo valid for **six months**. It is expected to achieve full MANCAP certification within that time. (SON-2)
- SON describes it as a way to make trade easier and encourage micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs). Contact the nearest SON state office to apply. (SON-2)
